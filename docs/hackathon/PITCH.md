# Pitch outline (3 minutes)

## 0:00 to 0:30 Problem
- Show of hands: who read the terms before clicking Agree today?
- Those terms decide if your data is sold, if your subscription renews, and what fees you pay. And they change after you agree.
- AI summaries exist, but how do you know the summary is right?

## 0:30 to 0:50 Solution
- Consent Receipts: a Chrome extension that gives you a receipt for every Agree click, a plain words risk summary, and an alert when the terms change.
- Every claim in the summary points to the exact sentence it came from.

## 0:50 to 2:20 Live demo (offline demo page, terms v1 and v2)
1. Click Agree on the demo site. A receipt appears in the popup.
2. Open the receipt: risk score, reasons, and click a claim to highlight its source sentence.
3. Simulate the terms change to v2 (Settings > Demo tools). Notification fires; show the diff of what changed.
4. Finance mode: show the flagged auto renewal and late fee (these terms only exist in v2).
5. What to do next: show the drafted cancel or opt out request.
- Backup: if anything fails, play the demo video.

## 2:20 to 2:45 Why it matters (FNB angle)
- Trust: you can verify every AI claim against the source text.
- Decisions: clear risk reasons and change alerts.
- Financial confidence: fees, rate changes, and renewals flagged before they cost you, with a next step ready.
- Private by design: everything stays in your browser.

## 2:45 to 3:00 What is next and close
- Privacy score across your browser, team compliance view.
- Close: "Read nothing, know everything you agreed to."

## Judge Q&A prep
- How accurate is the AI? Citations let users check every claim; rules fallback works without AI.
- Where does data go? Nowhere; local storage only. AI calls (if enabled) send only the policy text.
- What was built at HackPSU? See the Devpost prior work disclosure.
