How it works

Three steps, and the first one is the click you were going to make anyway.

  1. 1

    Click Agree like normal

    The extension notices Agree, Accept all, Allow, "I agree" boxes, and closed cookie notices, on regular pages and on OneTrust, Cookiebot, TrustArc, and Quantcast banners, even inside iframes. It saves a receipt in your browser.

  2. 2

    Read the receipt

    Open the popup for a plain words summary and a Low, Medium, or High risk score. Expand any point to see the exact policy sentence behind it. Receipts appear instantly from local rules and upgrade to an AI summary in the background.

  3. 3

    Act, and stay updated

    Get next steps with a drafted request you can copy, and a notification with a diff when a saved policy changes.

Built to be checked, not just trusted.

Every screen below is the real extension.

Cited summaries

Every point has a source.

Open any point to see the exact sentence from the policy behind it. The risk score lists its reasons: data sale, tracking, location, biometrics, retention, arbitration, auto renewal, AI training on your content, and terms that can change.

  • Negation aware rules: "we never sell your data" is not flagged
  • Tuned on real policies, not only fixtures
Finance mode

Money terms, flagged before they cost you.

Bank, loan, and subscription terms get their own detectors: fees, late payments, interest rate changes, overdraft, minimum balances, and auto renewals, each quoted from the document.

What to do next

A drafted request, ready to send.

Opt out of data sale or AI training, opt out of arbitration, cancel before a renewal, or request deletion. Each step shows the cited sentence and a drafted request you can copy, with a reminder before the renewal date.

Change alerts

See exactly what changed.

Saved policies are rechecked daily or on demand. When one changes you get a notification, the sentences added and removed, and the risk before and after.

Listen

One tap plays a short spoken summary of any receipt: Gemini writes it from the cited points and ElevenLabs reads it aloud. Free with an account.

Terms change tracker

A public page of policies that changed this week, built from anonymous reports. See what changed this week.

Private by design

Receipts stay in your browser. Without an account, nothing is sent to our server. With one, only the public policy text goes out, and never your browsing history.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Receipts, rule based summaries, change alerts, finance flags, and next actions work without an account, in your browser. Pro (Gemini summaries and Listen) is free too: you only need a free account.

Will I be charged?

No. Pro is free, and we never ask for a card. If that ever changes, we will tell you first and nothing will be charged unless you choose to pay.

What data leaves my browser?

Without an account, your receipts stay in your browser. With Pro on, the extension sends the public policy text to our server; summaries are cached per policy; we don't keep a record of which sites you visit.

Can I trust an AI summary?

You don't have to take it on faith. Every Gemini point must cite a sentence from the policy, points without a real source are dropped and lower the trust score, and the rule based findings are always shown alongside.

How do I install it?

The Chrome Web Store listing is on its way. Until then, download it from the install page and load it in about a minute. Chrome, Edge, Brave, and other Chromium browsers work.

Is this legal advice?

No. Consent Receipts helps you read and keep track of terms. For a legal question, talk to a lawyer.
