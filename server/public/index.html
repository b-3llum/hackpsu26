A receipt for every Agree.

Consent Receipts is a Chrome extension. When you click Agree, Accept all, Allow, or tick an "I agree" box, it saves a receipt with the site, the time, the button you clicked, the policy links, and the cookie categories. Then it reads the terms for you, scores the risk as Low, Medium, or High, and links every point back to the sentence it came from.

Nobody reads the terms. They still count.

Terms of service, cookie banners, and bank or subscription agreements decide whether your data is sold, whether a plan renews on its own, and which fees you can be charged. They are long, they are written for lawyers, and they can change quietly after you sign up. Most of us click Agree and hope for the best.

How it works

Three steps, and the first one is the click you were going to make anyway.

01

Click Agree like normal

The extension notices consent clicks on regular pages and on OneTrust, Cookiebot, and TrustArc banners, and saves a receipt in your browser.

02

Read the receipt

Open the popup to see a plain words summary and a risk score. Expand any point to see the exact policy sentence behind it.

03

Act, and stay updated

Get suggested next steps with a drafted request you can copy, and a notice with a diff when a saved policy changes.

Features

Built to be checked, not just trusted.

Cited summaries

Every point in a summary expands to the policy sentence it came from, so you can verify it yourself.

Risk score with reasons

Low, Medium, or High, with the reasons listed: data sale, tracking, location, biometrics, retention, arbitration, auto renewal, AI training on your content, and terms that can change.

Finance mode

Flags fees, interest rate changes, overdraft terms, late payments, minimum balances, and auto renewals in bank, loan, and subscription terms.

Change alerts

Saved policies are checked daily or on demand. When one changes, you get a notice and a diff of what changed.

What to do next

Suggested actions such as opting out of data sale, AI training, or arbitration, canceling before a renewal, or requesting deletion, each with a drafted request and the cited sentence.

Gemini summaries on Pro

Gemini has to cite a numbered policy sentence for every point. Points without a real source are dropped and lower a visible trust score, and the rule based findings always stay.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Receipts, rule based summaries, change alerts, finance flags, and next actions are free and run in your browser. Pro is $3 per month and adds Gemini summaries.

Will I be charged?

Not during HackPSU Fall 26. Checkout runs in Stripe test mode, so no real payment is taken.

What data leaves my browser?

On Free, your receipts stay in your browser. Pro sends the public policy text to our server; summaries are cached per policy; we don't keep a record of which sites you visit.

Can I trust an AI summary?

You don't have to take it on faith. Every Gemini point must cite a sentence from the policy, points without a real source are dropped and lower the trust score, and the rule based findings are always shown alongside.

Is this legal advice?

No. Consent Receipts helps you read and keep track of terms. For a legal question, talk to a lawyer.

Which browsers does it support?

Chrome, as a Manifest V3 extension.
