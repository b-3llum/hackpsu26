Privacy Policy

HackPSU Fall 26 project, not legal advice. Last updated October 24, 2026.

In short

Pro sends the public policy text to our server; summaries are cached per policy; policy change reports are anonymous; we don't keep a record of which sites you visit.

Data that stays in your browser

Your receipts, the sites they came from, rule based summaries, change alerts, finance flags, and suggested actions are stored locally by the extension. They are not sent to us. You can export them from the extension.

What your account stores

  • Your name and email address.
  • A scrypt hash of your password, if you sign up with email and password. We never store the password itself.
  • If you choose Continue with Google, the verified email and name Google shares through Auth0, used to sign you in to the same account. We don't get your Google password.
  • Your plan (Free or Pro), your subscription status, and your renewal date.
  • Your Stripe customer ID, if you start Pro checkout.
  • If you connect the extension, a sign in token stored in the extension. Our server keeps only a hash of that token.
  • A session cookie that keeps you signed in.

Pro summaries

When you ask for a Pro summary, the extension sends the public text of that policy to our server, which asks Gemini for a cited summary. Summaries are cached by policy so the same policy isn't summarized twice. We don't keep a record of which sites you visit.

Listen to this receipt

When you press Listen, our server sends the summary text (the plain words summary and flagged clauses we show you, not the page itself) to ElevenLabs to turn it into speech. The audio is cached per summary so the same summary isn't sent twice. We count plays per account to apply the free daily limit, but we don't keep a record of which sites you visit.

Terms change tracker

When the extension notices that a policy changed, it reports an anonymous change event to our server: the policy address without query strings, a hash of the policy text, the risk level before and after, and the time. Events are stored in Tiger Data (Timescale) and power the public What changed this week page. They carry no user id, account, or IP address, so they can't be tied back to you.

Hosting

The website runs behind Cloudflare, which processes requests to deliver it and sees IP addresses like any content delivery network. Our app does not keep request logs.

Service providers

We use these services only for the features above: Google Gemini (summaries), ElevenLabs (Listen), Tiger Data (anonymous change events), Auth0 (Continue with Google), Stripe (payments), and Cloudflare (hosting). Each feature is off unless we have set it up, and none of them receives your receipts.

Payments

Stripe processes checkout and billing. We don't see or store your card number. During HackPSU Fall 26, checkout runs in Stripe test mode.

Cookies

The website uses one session cookie to keep you signed in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

Remove the extension to delete everything stored in your browser. To delete your account, contact the project team through the repository.
